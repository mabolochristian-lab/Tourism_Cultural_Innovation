A LIVING TRADITION

The Moriones
Cultural Spotlight

More than a festival, the Moriones is a tradition carried by the people of Marinduque.

During Holy Week, participants don vivid Roman-inspired masks and costumes as they reenact the story of Longinus. Known locally as Moryonan, this centuries-old observance brings faith, artistry, and community together across the island.

WHENHoly Week
WHEREAcross Marinduque
EXPERIENCEObserve with respect
